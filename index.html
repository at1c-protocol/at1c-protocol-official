Four steps. One receipt. Permanent proof.

02 — APPROVE

03 — PROOF

AT1C Registry

Every agent has a registered identity. Every action has a signed receipt.

Before an AI agent can act on your behalf, it needs an identity. The AT1C Registry is where that identity lives — public, verifiable, and owned by you. Register your agent and it receives a signed certificate. Every action it takes from that moment produces a cryptographic receipt.
